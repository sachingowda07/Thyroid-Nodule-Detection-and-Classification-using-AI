Thyroid Nodule Information

Learn about different types of thyroid nodules detected by our AI system.

🟢 Benign Nodule
Risk Level: Very Low
Benign nodules are non-cancerous growths. They usually require periodic follow-up but are not a major health concern.
🟡 Stage 1 Nodule
Risk Level: Low
Early-stage nodule with mild tissue irregularity. Follow-up imaging is suggested to ensure no abnormal growth.
🟠 Stage 2 Nodule
Risk Level: Moderate
Indicates increased growth activity. Medical consultation and thyroid function tests may be recommended.
🔴 Stage 3 Nodule
Risk Level: High
High-risk nodule showing strong signs of malignancy. Immediate clinical evaluation is strongly advised.
