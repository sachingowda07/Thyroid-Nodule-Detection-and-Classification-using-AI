About ThyroidDetect

AI-Powered Thyroid Ultrasound Classification & Medical Decision Support

💡

Our Mission

To make thyroid disorder detection fast, reliable, and accessible using cutting-edge deep-learning technology trained on real medical ultrasound datasets.

🧠

How Our AI Works

The system analyzes uploaded ultrasound images to classify nodules into Benign, Stage 1, Stage 2, or Stage 3 using a custom-trained EfficientNet-B0 model.

🔬

Medical Value

Early detection improves patient outcomes. ThyroidDetect assists doctors by providing an instant risk-assessment based on thousands of training samples.

🔒

Your Privacy

All uploaded images and patient data stay securely stored inside your device or project database only. No external servers or cloud uploads.
